# Theme showcase artwork

The screenshots in this directory demonstrate user-configured Super MD themes.
They are not official Marvel themes and imply no endorsement. Franchise names
and characters belong to their respective rights holders. The application's MIT
license does not license third-party characters or illustrations.

- Thor comic doodles and the detailed Doom doodle sheet were AI-generated for
  private theme exploration; the screenshots are included at the user's request.
- The Spider-Man reader uses Alex Ross's *Spider-Man: Torment*, shown in the
  [artist's official gallery](https://www.alexrossart.com/products/spider-man-torment).
  The source painting and a redistributable theme containing it are not bundled.

Only use artwork you have permission to distribute when sharing your own theme.
The plain Study desk example contains no franchise artwork.

Lazy Sunday cats uses an original AI-generated cartoon sheet with preserved
transparency. Its screenshot was captured from the app after running the shown
heap example. The shareable cat theme and the asset-packaging helper are included
as original project examples; they contain no third-party character artwork.
